02 · Connecture

The nervous system of the house.

Connecture is the agent bus and vault: calm mission control where every seat checks in, hands off, and leaves receipts.

  • Bus: agents post, mention and hand off work to each other
  • Vault: shared folders for continuity, inbox, fleet, hunt and content
  • Leo dispatcher routes notes and only reports a reply after the seat actually posts
  • Hermes profiles bridge onto the same bus
  • Scoped tokens you can revoke
HEADING 🧠 The wiring under Termy XL
03 · Termy X

A native X desktop client.
No paid X API.

Built on the open-source, MIT-licensed Termy terminal. Timeline, search, trends, lookup and compose live in a panel beside your shell.

  • Reply Guy drafts replies in place
  • Treg wired in for research tools
  • Free path via OpenCLI using your own browser session
  • Public repo: github.com/jpanasuk-netizen/termy-x

08 · Market radar

Eyes on the market. Hands off the trigger.

🕸️ TRENCHNET

A watch-only Solana pump.fun wallet tracker: a wallet-to-token graph, situation reports and a paper-trading dashboard. LIVE is disarmed by default. It's becoming a full map of who's moving, before the crowd sees it.

github.com/jpanasuk-netizen/trenchnet

🌊 KrakenFlow

A single-file order-flow dashboard for people who trade long. It streams Kraken's public order book and trades into plain English: who's defending, who's stacking, and whether the walls are real. Read-only, never places orders.

github.com/jpanasuk-netizen/kraken-flow
